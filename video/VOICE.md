# English narration and bilingual captions

This is the narration source for the English product film. The voice is **AI-generated** using Kokoro's standard `af_heart` American English voice. It does not clone or imitate a particular person.

- [Script](narration-en.json): English spoken copy, Chinese translation and intended display windows.
- [Narration master](assets/narration-en.flac): 60-second lossless mono track, with speech placed at the recorded scene times.
- [Measured timings](voice-timing.json): actual speech duration, playback speed and narration hash for each cue.
- [Bilingual SRT](scriber-en-bilingual.srt) · [English SRT](scriber-en-en.srt) · [Chinese SRT](scriber-en-zh.srt). Matching VTT files are also supplied.

The final film burns both languages into the picture: English above Chinese. The separate subtitle files are provided for editing and accessibility. The narration is synthesized per cue so it follows the demonstration rather than running ahead of it; clips must fit their allotted windows and narration speed is capped.

## Regenerate locally

```sh
uv venv video/.venv --python 3.11
uv pip install --python video/.venv/bin/python -r video/requirements-voice.txt
video/.venv/bin/python video/narrate.py --download-models
```

The first run downloads approximately 121 MB of public model and voice data into ignored `video/output/voice-models/`. URLs, lengths and SHA256 digests are pinned in [voice-sources.json](voice-sources.json). Later runs validate and reuse the files. No script or captured audio is uploaded to a voice service. Regeneration can vary with runtime versions; the committed master and timings identify this production.

The original [Kokoro-82M model](https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M) is Apache-2.0; the ONNX conversion and voice package come from the maintainer's [v1.0 model release](https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx/releases/tag/model-files-v1.0). We do not redistribute model weights. Original instrumental music remains generated by `music.py` and is mixed below the narration.

## Voice checks

The lossless master decodes to exactly 60 seconds at 24 kHz, mono. All 13 speech clips are nonempty and end within their paired subtitle windows. This production uses speed 0.95 throughout; no cue required faster delivery. The final clip ends before the outro finishes.

An independent local Whisper tiny.en transcription recovered every statement, including the Scriber name. Its approximate transcript is retained in [voice-asr.txt](voice-asr.txt); the spoken letters “M four A” appear as the homophone “M for a” in ASR output. This is a content/intelligibility check, not a claim of human listening or exact ASR word timing. The original files are preserved by their hashes.
